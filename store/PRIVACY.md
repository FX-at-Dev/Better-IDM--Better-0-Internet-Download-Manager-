# Prism browser extension — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

The Prism browser extension connects your browser to the **Prism download manager app installed on your own computer**. It does not send anything to the developer or to any third party.

## What the extension handles

| Data | When | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| The address (URL) of a file you download, its file name, and the page it came from | When a browser download starts, or you choose "Download with Prism" | The Prism app on your computer (`127.0.0.1`) |
| Cookies for that file's website and your browser's user-agent string | Same as above, so downloads from sites you're signed in to keep working | The Prism app on your computer |
| The address of the page you're on | When you click **Download video** or "Download video with Prism" | The Prism app on your computer |
| Addresses of audio/video files the page loads | While you browse, to list them in the extension's popup | Kept in the browser's memory for that tab only; sent to Prism only if you click **Get** |
| Settings (local port, optional access token) | When you change them | Your browser's local extension storage |

All communication is with `http://127.0.0.1` — your own computer. Nothing leaves your device through the extension.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
- No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
- No remote code is loaded; everything runs from the extension package.

## What the Prism app does with it

The Prism app uses the information only to download the files you asked for, and keeps your download list on your computer. You can remove downloads from the list, or uninstall Prism, at any time.
